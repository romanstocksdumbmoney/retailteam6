Sign in / create account first

How AI gets implemented here

  • When you click I finished signup - Auto Implement AI, the app saves your AI bot defaults directly to your user profile.
  • It sets your trading mode to Live (with hands-free execution still off by default for safety).
  • It preloads risk controls: risk per trade, reward/risk minimum, max positions, and sector limits.
  • It then opens Broker Connect preselected for your broker so you can finish credentials + bridge test.

After this, open AI Brokerage Account View to see exactly what the AI is amplifying and proposing.

What the AI amplifies

  • AI Trade Queue: chart setups you submitted from AI Trade.
  • Trend Trades: social momentum signals.
  • High IV Tracker: names with elevated options volatility context.
  • Prompt priorities: symbols/sectors/direction you told AI to favor or avoid.
  • Risk/Reward filter: only proposals that pass your minimum reward/risk rule.
