Set Up Trading Account with AI

Follow this page to get AI connected to your account so it can plan and execute trades based on your configured rules.

Direct Broker Signup + Auto AI Setup

What this setup does

  • Configures AI strategy + risk controls.
  • Sets funding mode for live account trading.
  • Connects broker bridge and verifies it.
  • Lets you run AI with manual approvals or hands-free live mode.

Important: brokerage signup/KYC/2FA must be completed by the account owner.

Recommended broker API path: Interactive Brokers (TWS/Gateway API) is a widely respected choice for automation workflows, while Robinhood/Webull support can vary by integration approach.
