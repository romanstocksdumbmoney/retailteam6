Is my money safe?

Yes. Your funds stay in your brokerage account at all times. DumbDollars only sends trade instructions — it never holds your money.

Can I use paper trading first?

Yes. Alpaca offers free paper trading with $100,000 in simulated funds. We strongly recommend testing there before going live.

How does the AI get access to trade?

You generate an API key in your broker account and paste it into DumbDollars. This gives the AI permission to trade on your behalf.
